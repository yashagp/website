PatwardhanMed Consulting offers comprehensive regulatory and quality consulting services to medical device and pharmaceutical manufacturers worldwide.

Founded by Yasha Patwardhan in February 2022, PatwardhanMed Consulting specializes in providing high-quality consulting services for regulatory compliance and Quality Management Systems (QMS) to medical device and pharmaceutical manufacturers. We prioritize a client-centric approach that begins with active listening, thorough learning, and comprehensive analysis to deliver tailored solutions. We recognize that there is no one-size-fits-all strategy for streamlining risk management and QMS processes while maintaining regulatory compliance. With over 15 years of experience dedicated to client satisfaction, our goal is to deliver sustainable, results-oriented outcomes.

Our Philosophy


Customer satisfaction is our top priority. However, the greatest value we bring as consultants lies in our extensive network of trusted professionals and partners. We ensure that our clients never have to "clean up" after another consultant's work, as poorly implemented changes and projects can damage both professional trust and our clients' long-term success. Our approach is straightforward: 'If we would not cook it in our kitchen, we won't recommend it to anyone else. Ever'.
